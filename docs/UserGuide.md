# Q-Med User Guide

Welcome to Q-Med! 

## For Patients
- **Voice Booking:** Click the microphone icon on the ChatBot to describe your symptoms. The AI will recommend a hospital and department and book an appointment for you!
- **Dashboard:** View your live queue position, estimated wait time, and medical history (prescriptions & files).

## For Doctors
- **Queue Management:** See exactly who is waiting in your queue. Call the next patient with a single click.
- **Prescriptions:** Write consultation notes, prescribe medicines, and attach lab reports. Completing a prescription automatically updates the patient's records.

## For Receptionists
- **Walk-ins:** Generate tokens for walk-in patients immediately.
- **Queue Control:** View and manage the queues for all doctors in the hospital.
